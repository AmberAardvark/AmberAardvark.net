## Onboarding Steps

<Steps>

1. Discovery Call Kick Off With a Discovery Session
   We'll walk through your current systems, pain points, and goals to scope the right engagement.

2. Access & Scoping Grant Access and Confirm Scope
   Share access to the relevant systems and data sources, and we'll confirm the scope, timeline, and success criteria in writing.

3. Delivery & Handover Build, Review, and Hand Off
   We build iteratively with regular check-ins, then document and hand off everything your team needs to run it independently.

</Steps>

## Ongoing Support & Maintenance

<Aside type="tip">
  Keep credentials and access reviewed on a regular schedule, and let us know
  early if source systems change so pipelines and agents keep working.
</Aside>

Regular check-ins keep pipelines, automations, and agents reliable. We monitor for issues after delivery and are available for updates as your data sources and requirements evolve.
