Amber Aardvark's Numbat platform is built from a curated set of best-in-class open-source components. These resources aren't just about explaining what each piece does — they're about helping you configure and run them with confidence. Below, you will find a curated selection of guides geared towards helping you get the most out of the Numbat stack.

### Component Guides

**Storage & Transformation Guide**

- Detailed setup and configuration for PostgreSQL, MinIO, Apache Iceberg, DuckDB, and Polars
- Visual guide for choosing the right storage layer for your workload

**Orchestration Handbook**

- Instructions on scheduling and monitoring pipelines with Dagster
- Tips on structuring data quality checks with Pandera

**Analytics & Apps Catalog**

- Exploring Cube, Apache Superset, Vega-Lite/D3.js, and Appsmith for semantic modeling, dashboards, and custom apps
- Guidelines for selecting the right visualization or app layer for your use case

**Governance & Security Instructions**

- Comprehensive procedures for identity, secrets, and lineage using Authentik, OpenBao, and DataHub
- Recommendations for access policies and audit practices
